# Research and qualification

## Purpose

Use before first contact or when a lead lacks useful context.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Confirm the business, likely need, contact context and one relevant question.

## Copyable example

> What are you trying to improve at the moment, and how are you handling it today?

## What not to say

Do not pretend to know the prospect’s problems or claim that Devign has guaranteed results.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the research facts, source, qualification outcome and outreach status.

## Recommended next action

Choose a suitable contact channel or mark the lead as not a fit.

## When to stop following up

Stop researching when there is enough information for a respectful first contact, or close the lead when there is no fit.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
