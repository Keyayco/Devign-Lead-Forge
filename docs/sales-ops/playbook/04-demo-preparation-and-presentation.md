# Demo preparation and presentation

## Purpose

Use when a demo is appropriate and the prospect has agreed.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Use the prospect’s stated problem, audience and desired outcome.

## Copyable example

> Based on what you shared about [problem], I’ll show only the parts relevant to [outcome]. Please stop me if this does not match your workflow.

## What not to say

Do not show irrelevant features, hide limitations or promise unapproved implementation terms.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the demo stage, questions, objections, response and agreed next action.

## Recommended next action

Send the agreed material only when appropriate and move the demo status to Sent after sending.

## When to stop following up

Stop presenting when the prospect has enough information to decide the next step.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
