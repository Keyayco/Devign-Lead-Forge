# Requests for free work

## Purpose

Use when a prospect asks for unpaid work, a full audit or a speculative build.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Clarify the requested outcome and explain the approved boundary.

## Copyable example

> I can explain the approach and show relevant examples, but a custom piece of work would need an agreed scope. Would you like to discuss that scope?

## What not to say

Do not promise a free build, hide the amount of work or imply that payment is already agreed.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the request and the boundary explained.

## Recommended next action

Offer an approved paid or clearly bounded next step if one exists.

## When to stop following up

Stop when the prospect only wants unpaid delivery or will not accept a clear scope.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
