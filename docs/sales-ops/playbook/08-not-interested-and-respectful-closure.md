# Not interested and respectful closure

## Purpose

Use when a prospect declines.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Acknowledge the answer without arguing.

## Copyable example

> Thanks for letting me know. I’ll close this off and won’t keep following up. If your situation changes, you’re welcome to reach out.

## What not to say

Do not challenge the reason, guilt the prospect or ask for a referral immediately.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record not interested and any useful reason the prospect offered.

## Recommended next action

Move on and keep the closure factual.

## When to stop following up

Stop immediately when the prospect says no or asks not to be contacted.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
