# Negotiation, scope and closing

## Purpose

Use when the prospect is considering a decision.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Confirm the outcome, scope, decision-maker and unresolved terms.

## Copyable example

> It sounds as though the next decision is [decision]. Shall we confirm the scope and the remaining question before deciding whether to proceed?

## What not to say

Do not change contract terms, revision limits or payment schedules without approval.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the decision stage, scope questions and owner of the next action.

## Recommended next action

Use the approved commercial documents when available and request internal approval for exceptions.

## When to stop following up

Stop when the prospect is not proceeding or when no legitimate next step is agreed.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
