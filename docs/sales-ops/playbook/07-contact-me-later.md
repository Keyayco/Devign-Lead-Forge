# Contact me later

## Purpose

Use when the prospect gives a future timeframe.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Record the requested timeframe and the reason only if the prospect volunteered it.

## Copyable example

> Of course. I’ll make a note to reconnect around [timeframe]. If that no longer suits you, you can simply let me know.

## What not to say

Do not treat “later” as permission for unlimited contact or invent a fixed appointment.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the requested timing and outreach status. Dedicated reminders are future functionality.

## Recommended next action

Set your approved reminder outside Lead Forge if the team permits, then update the lead when contact happens.

## When to stop following up

Stop if the later contact is declined or receives no response after the agreed attempt.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
