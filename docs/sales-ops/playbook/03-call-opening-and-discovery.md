# Call opening and discovery

## Purpose

Use when a prospect has agreed to speak.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Personalise the reason for the call and confirm the available time.

## Copyable example

> Thanks for making time. I’ll keep this focused. May I first understand how you handle [area] today, then we can decide whether it is worth showing anything?

## What not to say

Do not launch into a long pitch, interrupt repeatedly or imply the call has a result already.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the problem, current approach, desired outcome, decision process and next step.

## Recommended next action

Summarise what you heard and ask whether a focused demo would help.

## When to stop following up

Stop when the prospect says the topic is not relevant or the agreed call has ended without a next step.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
