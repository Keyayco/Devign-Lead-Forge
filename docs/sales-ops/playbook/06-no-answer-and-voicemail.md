# No-answer and voicemail

## Purpose

Use after a missed call when a voicemail is appropriate.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Keep it brief and include your name, reason and an easy return route.

## Copyable example

> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from Devign. I’m calling about [brief reason]. You can reach me on [approved channel]. No need to call back if this is not relevant.

## What not to say

Do not leave repeated urgent messages or imply an existing relationship that does not exist.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the attempt, channel and whether a voicemail was left.

## Recommended next action

Wait and use the approved follow-up window.

## When to stop following up

Stop after the team’s agreed no-answer sequence or an explicit request not to call.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
