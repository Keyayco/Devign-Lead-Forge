# Trust, timing, existing provider and DIY objections

## Purpose

Use when the prospect is unsure, busy, already supported or considering doing it themselves.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Personalise the response to the actual concern and ask one clarifying question.

## Copyable example

> That is reasonable. What would you need to see or understand before deciding whether an outside partner is useful?

## What not to say

Do not criticise an existing provider, dismiss DIY, create fear or claim Devign is the only option.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the concern, evidence requested and agreed next step.

## Recommended next action

Offer a small, clear next conversation only if it is useful.

## When to stop following up

Stop when the prospect is satisfied with another option or does not want further contact.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
