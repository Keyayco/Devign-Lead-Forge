# Handover after a sale

## Purpose

Use once a sale is confirmed and another teammate needs to continue.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Include the agreed outcome, scope, contacts, commitments and risks.

## Copyable example

> Here is the handover summary: the agreed outcome is [outcome], the current scope is [scope], the next milestone is [milestone], and the open question is [question].

## What not to say

Do not hand over vague promises, private credentials or unapproved commitments.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the sale status, handover owner, commitments and next action.

## Recommended next action

Confirm the receiving teammate has the information needed to begin.

## When to stop following up

Stop contacting the prospect as the primary owner once the handover is accepted, unless asked to assist.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
