# Price and affordability

## Purpose

Use when cost is raised before quoting approved terms.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Understand whether the concern is budget, timing, scope or uncertainty.

## Copyable example

> That makes sense. Is the concern the total budget, the timing, or whether the outcome would justify the investment?

## What not to say

Do not invent discounts, prices, payment plans or guaranteed return.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the objection and what information is still needed.

## Recommended next action

Use only the current approved pricing source and confirm scope with the responsible person.

## When to stop following up

Stop negotiating when the approved scope cannot meet the need or the prospect declines.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
