# Post-demo follow-up

## Purpose

Use after a completed demo.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Mention the agreed outcome, open question and next decision.

## Copyable example

> Thanks for the conversation. You mentioned [priority]. The useful next step seems to be [next step]. Would [option] be helpful, or should we leave this for now?

## What not to say

Do not pretend the prospect agreed to buy or create artificial urgency.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the demo outcome, open questions and follow-up status.

## Recommended next action

Complete the agreed next step or close the loop.

## When to stop following up

Stop when the prospect declines, asks not to be contacted or does not engage after the agreed attempts.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
