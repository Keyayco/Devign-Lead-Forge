# Personalised first-contact messages

## Purpose

Use for a first message after basic research.

## When to use it

Use this guidance when the situation matches the purpose above. Keep the conversation suitable for the prospect and channel.

## Information to personalise

Personalise the name, business context and reason for contacting.

## Copyable example

> Hi [Name], I noticed [specific business context]. We help teams improve [relevant outcome]. Would it be useful to compare notes for 15 minutes, or is someone else better placed to speak with?

## What not to say

Do not use a mass message, fake familiarity, pressure or an unsupported promise.

## What to record in Lead Forge

Record the channel, date, message purpose and response or no-response outcome.

## Recommended next action

Wait for a reply before sending the next relevant message.

## When to stop following up

Stop after the agreed number of respectful attempts or immediately when asked not to be contacted.

Scripts are guidance, not automatic messages. Adapt them honestly and do not send them without checking the recipient, channel and context.
