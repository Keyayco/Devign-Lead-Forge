# Follow-ups and stopping rules

Lead Forge currently lets you record follow-up context in notes and outreach status. Dedicated scheduled reminders are **future functionality** and are not currently part of the application.

A respectful follow-up:

- Refers to the previous conversation.
- Adds a useful reason to reply.
- Offers a simple next step.
- Gives the prospect room to decline.

If someone says no, asks not to be contacted, or repeatedly does not engage, record the outcome and stop according to the team’s agreed practice. Do not keep sending messages simply because a lead remains in the queue.
