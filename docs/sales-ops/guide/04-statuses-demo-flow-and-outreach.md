# Statuses, demos, FLOW and outreach

## Lead status

- **Finessing:** still being prepared or worked.
- **Sold:** closed successfully.
- **Cold:** no response or not interested.
- **Pipeline customer:** a returning or continuing customer.

## Demo status

- **None:** no demo is planned or linked.
- **Building:** the demo is being prepared.
- **Ready:** the demo is ready to present.
- **Sent:** the demo or link has been sent.

## Outreach status

- **Not started:** no contact attempt is recorded.
- **Contacted:** an attempt has been made.
- **Responded:** the prospect has replied.
- **Follow-up:** a next contact is needed.

## FLOW

FLOW provides a deterministic indication of the next useful action, such as acting now, preparing, or completing. Treat it as a prompt, not as a replacement for the lead context or a promise of success.
