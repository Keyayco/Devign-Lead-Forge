# Getting started

## Sign in

Open the Lead Forge web address and sign in with your Devign account. The authenticated workspace appears after the saved session is restored.

## Find your way around

The **Leads workspace** is the main queue. On a phone, use the menu button in the top bar to open navigation. On a wider screen, use the sidebar. The workspace shows search, filters, lead cards or a desktop table, and lead actions.

## Online and offline use

Previously loaded leads can remain available offline on a device that has already been authenticated. Search and existing filters can work against the cached queue. Claiming remains unavailable offline. Restore connectivity before protected server actions and before signing out.

## First useful action

Open a lead, read the brief, check the current owner and status, then decide whether you are researching, contacting, following up or handing it over.
