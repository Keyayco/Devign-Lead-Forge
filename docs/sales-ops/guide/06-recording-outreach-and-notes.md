# Recording outreach and notes

Record what happened, when it happened and what was agreed.

A useful note answers:

- What did we learn?
- What did the prospect say or ask?
- What did we promise?
- What is the next action?
- When should contact stop if there is no response?

Avoid passwords, payment details, unnecessary personal information and unsupported claims. Keep notes short enough to scan on an Android phone. Update outreach status after a real outcome, not merely because you planned to contact someone.
