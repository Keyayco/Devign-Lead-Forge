# Research and qualification

Before first contact, learn enough to avoid a generic message.

1. Confirm the business name and what it appears to do.
2. Identify a plausible need that Devign may address.
3. Note the source of your information and the date if it may change.
4. Identify the right contact context without guessing private details.
5. Prepare one useful question.

Qualification is a conversation, not a checklist to force. If there is no fit, record that clearly and close respectfully.
