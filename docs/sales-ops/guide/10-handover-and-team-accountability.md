# Handover and team accountability

A good handover lets the next person continue without starting again.

Before handover, record:

- Current owner and why ownership is changing.
- Latest contact and outcome.
- Current lead, demo and outreach statuses.
- Outstanding questions.
- Agreed next action and any stopping point.

Tell the receiving teammate directly when a decision or deadline matters. Do not silently leave a claimed lead with no useful next step.
