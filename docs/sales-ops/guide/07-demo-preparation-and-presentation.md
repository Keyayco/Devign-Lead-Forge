# Demo preparation and presentation

Prepare the demo around the prospect’s stated problem.

1. Review the lead brief and notes.
2. Confirm the outcome you want from the meeting.
3. Show only relevant workflows.
4. Explain what is available today and what is not.
5. Invite questions and check understanding.
6. Agree the next step and record it.

Do not invent results, testimonials, features, prices or commitments. Move the demo status from Building to Ready only when it is genuinely ready, and to Sent only after it has been sent.
