# Purpose and the Devign sales process

Lead Forge is Devign’s shared workspace for capturing, understanding and progressing leads. It is not an automatic messaging system and it does not replace judgement.

## The practical flow

1. Find or create a lead with enough context for another teammate to understand it.
2. Research the business before contacting it.
3. Claim a lead only when you are ready to take responsibility for the next action.
4. Record truthful notes, status and outreach outcomes.
5. Keep the next step clear, then hand over cleanly when ownership changes.

## Good team practice

Write notes so a teammate can act without asking you to repeat the whole conversation. Record facts, not guesses. Respect a prospect’s time and stop contacting them when they clearly ask you to stop.
